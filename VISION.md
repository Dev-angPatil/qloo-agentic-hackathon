# 🎯 VISION

## The Problem

AI agents can reason, plan, and execute — but they are **culturally blind**. Ask them what to recommend, what to build, or what people actually want, and they're guessing. They lack the deep understanding of human taste and cultural affinity that makes recommendations go from "technically functional" to "actually good."

## The Solution

Build an agentic tool / agent-powered application that integrates **Qloo's Cultural AI** and **Taste Graph** (250M+ entities) to give AI agents real **cultural grounding**. The agent should outperform generic LLM-only approaches because it has access to Qloo's deep taste intelligence.
